As played by

An MEI customization for recording how a score was played: which written note each sounded event realised, when it was struck, how loud, and where the two disagreed. The record lives in <performance> and travels with the score.

Below, the same music is drawn twice. The upper score is laid out by notated duration, the way any renderer draws it. The lower one is laid out by the times a performance actually took, read out of a <recording> in the same file.

As played

Noteheads stand where they were struck rather than where the metre puts them, so the barlines are drawn dashed: they no longer sit at a fixed distance. A note the recording never reached is bracketed.

The performed layout is not part of verovio upstream. It comes from the aligned-mei branch of the fork, whose build is committed in this repository. The alignment itself is made in the MPM Desk.
